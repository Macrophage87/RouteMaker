Acknowledgements

RouteMaker stands on other people's work: the volunteers who map the region in OpenStreetMap, the public agencies that publish their data, the researchers whose methods it follows, and the open source projects it is built from. Thank you.

At a glance

Every source below is listed with what RouteMaker uses it for and its licence. The full record, with dataset ids, download dates and checksums, is in docs/SOURCES.md. The licence texts of the software bundled into the app are in Software licences.
