Welcome, tester

RouteMaker plans calm bike routes from DC to Baltimore, and talks you through them. Free, open source, still in testing.

No download needed: RouteMaker works in any browser, on a phone or a computer. The Android app adds voice cues that keep going with the screen locked.

Open the one for your device, then Everyone:

Android
  1. Join the testers group (tap Join group).
  2. Opt in on the testing page (tap Become a tester).
  3. Install RouteMaker from Google Play, open it, and press Go.

Or skip the app and just use the browser.

At your first ride the app may say your phone can stop it. Press Set battery to Unrestricted and choose Unrestricted on the page it opens, or cues can go quiet mid-ride. More: Join the Android beta.

iPhone
  1. There is no iPhone app yet, so open this site in Safari, not inside another app.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open RouteMaker from there.
  3. Allow location while using the app.

Go, Plan and the ride's voice cues work. Cues stop when the screen locks, so RouteMaker asks to keep the screen on during a ride and tells you if it can't (then set Auto-Lock to Never).

Computer

Open the map in any browser to plan routes, or download one for a bike computer.

Everyone
  • Something went wrong? Settings, About, Send feedback. No account needed. Or tell us on Discord.
  • What's new: the changelog channel on Discord.
  • When something is down, a short line at the top of Go and Plan says so, such as "Updating. Back in a few minutes." No line means it is working, unless you are offline.
  • Screen readers and other access needs: Accessibility.
