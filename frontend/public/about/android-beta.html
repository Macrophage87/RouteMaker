Join the Android beta

RouteMaker is a free, open source route planning and navigation tool for a wide variety of cycling applications. The Android app is in testing on Google Play, and you can join in three steps.

Do these on your phone, or anywhere you are signed in to the same Google account as your phone's Play Store.

  1. Join the testers group. Open the RouteMaker testers group, sign in to Google if it asks, and tap Join group.
  2. Opt in on Google Play. Open the RouteMaker testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install the app. On your phone, open RouteMaker on Google Play and tap Install. If Play says the app isn't available yet, wait a few minutes and try again.

Please stay in for at least 14 days. Google lets us release the app to everyone only after 12 testers have stayed opted in for 14 days in a row, so keep the app installed and stay in the group until then.

Found a problem?

Tell us on Discord or email stephen@routemakerdc.com. What you tapped and what you expected helps most.

Leaving the test

Open the testing page, tap Leave the program, then uninstall the app.
