**New**
- Claude on the web and on your phone can plan and save RouteMaker routes for you: add RouteMaker as a connector and sign in with Discord. Settings, Account, AI assistants lists each app you've connected, with Disconnect.
- Routes an assistant plans open exactly as planned from its share link, and it can hand you a GPX file.
- Star your favorite ride types and they're listed first whenever you choose a ride type, in Go's More ride types and in Plan. Signed in, they follow you to your other devices.

**Coming with this map update**
- Parking aisles and driveways open to bikes are drawn on the stress map, such as Pennyfield Lock Road.
- Roads Maryland's highway agency (MDOT SHA) closes to bikes are left off the map and out of routes.
- Roads Montgomery County rates LTS 5 show as LTS 4.5, a very high-stress road, instead of Avoid.
- Traffic signals that DC, Maryland and Alexandria list but the map was missing now count as signals, so those crossings are no longer called unsignalised.
- Routes steer clear of cobblestones and rails in the road, such as Georgetown's O and P Streets and the H Street NE streetcar tracks. When a route does ride one, an amber triangle marks it, the directions name it, and Ride warns you ahead.
