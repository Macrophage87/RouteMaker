**RouteMaker 0.15.2**
- Favorites: Edit has Move up and Move down, so your saved places come in the order you choose (Home and Work stay first)
- Settings, My Go: "Default ride type" is now "Primary Go method": the way you usually get around, which Go opens in
- On a ride or in Roam with others sharing, a new Everyone button fits you and every other rider's dot on the map
- Shared rides: after a reload, Resume ride rejoins the shared ride, and a leader's link joins it instead of asking to resume
