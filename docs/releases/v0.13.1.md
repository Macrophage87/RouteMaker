**RouteMaker 0.13.1**
- More of the app in Spanish: Go, Ride, Roam, Settings and Plan's map bar
- Clearer turns: real U-turns are called U-turns, and a via point at a corner no longer hides the turn
- Ride warns early when your destination is across a busy road
- "Bike lane ends" only where the lane really ends
- Faster routes and loops; Route answers the moment you press it
- Save & load panel no longer jumps up on phones
- iPhone Home Screen app: the location message now points to the iPhone's Settings
