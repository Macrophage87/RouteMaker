**RouteMaker 0.13.1**
- Spanish reaches more of the app, including Settings, Roam and Plan
- Clearer turns: real U-turns are called U-turns, and a via point at a corner no longer hides the turn
- Ride says which side of the street each stop is on, and warns early when it's across a busy road
- On Full, Ride says where a bike lane ends for good and confirms long stretches ("Continue on X for 2 miles")
- Faster routes and loops; the Route button starts planning the moment you press it
- Save & load panel no longer jumps up on phones
- iPhone Home Screen app: the location message now points to the iPhone's Settings
