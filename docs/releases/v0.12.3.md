**New**
- Deer-management closures: a route through Rock Creek Park's closed roads or a Montgomery Parks park during its deer-management hours gets a "Check before you go" notice, only when the ride falls inside the closure dates and hours. Rock Creek uses last winter's dates (Nov 10 to Mar 31, nightly 6:45 pm to 4 am) until the National Park Service posts this winter's.

**AI assistants**
- An assistant's answer now says what the route rides before you open it: its roads in order with miles, stress level and designations (Montgomery County Rustic Roads, scenic byways, the East Coast Greenway, US Bicycle Routes), the stretches on stress level 3 and above, very-high-stress junctions and small detours.
- Ask for rustic or scenic roads in words: the assistant can list those within 5 mi (8 km) of a point, up to 15 mi (24 km), and route along them.
- Interval training: the assistant can check where efforts can run with no stop, signal, busy crossing or sharp turn, how many sets fit in one ride, and find climbs nearby for hill repeats.
- Closures: a plan lists the closures it goes through or round, and says when the route rides through one. Ask what is closed near a park or trail before you go.
- For an alleycat or errands, the assistant can keep your start and finish and ride the checkpoints between in the quickest order.
- A place you name is looked for near your other points, so "Moore Road" means the one near your ride. A via point reached by riding out and back is moved to where that spur leaves the road, when that makes the route shorter.
- RouteMaker's logo shows beside the connector in apps that read it (in Claude, once Google has picked it up).
