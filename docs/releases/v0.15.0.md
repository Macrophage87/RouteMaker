**RouteMaker 0.15.0**
- Share live, Group Ride and Mass Ride are sealed end to end on the phones, so even our server can't read the positions; ride links are shorter, with QR codes that scan more easily
- Riders' dots show at meetups and regroups, and pause after an hour in one place or at your saved Home
- Mass Ride routes now weigh how much room each road has for your ride size
- A warning when a stop or the end is on a road not open to the public, such as a base or private drive
- A phone set to one of the 19 draft languages opens Go and Ride in it, with no Settings step (Plan stays in English); dates and clock times follow the app's language
- Settings has Sign out everywhere; keeping a shared link Permanently or Until a date needs you signed in
- Link previews are ready the moment you share, and the app loads faster on phones
- After the next map update: roads open to bikes only at certain times follow those hours, and US 340 in West Virginia is rated 4.5
