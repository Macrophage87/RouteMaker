**RouteMaker 0.15.0**
- Broadcast is now called Share live (same button, beside Start ride), and its live positions are sealed on the phones, so the server cannot read them
- Mass Ride routes now take the road's room for your ride size into account
- Go: the trip time comes first, the route list starts closed, and every place row has one Ride here button; Grocery finds small grocers and "near me"
- Ride: larger street names, and a re-route keeps every stop still ahead, skipping only the next one when it would cost a long way round
- A phone set to one of the 19 draft languages opens Go and Ride in it, with no Settings step (Plan stays in English); dates and clock times follow the app's language
- Keep shared link "Permanently" or "Until a date" now needs you to be signed in
- After the next map update: roads open to bikes only at certain times, such as weekends, follow those hours
