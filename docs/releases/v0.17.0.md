**RouteMaker 0.17.0**
- Go: bike racks near where you're headed, offered as the ride's end and shown on the map; if a rack is full, tap for the next one
- Road ratings rechecked across the region; where ours differs from an agency's, the road panel's Why line says why
- Potential closures layer (was DC permits) adds planned work from DDOT, DC Water and WSSC; Maryland's county and town closures are now routed around
- Colors follow sunrise and sunset by default (Settings > Display > Colors), with a lighter dark map
- The route line marks protected and painted bike lanes
- Where to? finds Home, Work and your favorites by name, typed or said, plus coordinates and plus codes
- Fewer phantom turns and false "off route" alerts, and longer battery life on a ride
- All gravel is priced as packed gravel, and routes following an opened file stay on the road at corners
