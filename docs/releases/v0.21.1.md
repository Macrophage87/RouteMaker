**RouteMaker 0.21.1**
- Start a ride away from the route's start (more than about 650 ft and off the line) and the ride asks: Go to the start, or Join from here
- Go to the start makes the start your first stop, then rides on along the route as planned: a More direct or Calmer choice, a file followed exactly and a group's shared route ride unchanged
- Join from here re-routes you onto the route ahead, as when you leave it; within 650 ft of the start, or on the route part way along, the ride just goes on
