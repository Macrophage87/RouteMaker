RouteMaker 0.30.0
- Ride settings: one screen in Plan and Go, Traffic, Hills, Surface and When first, the rest under Advanced
- Surface: Paved only, Paved and gravel, Easy MTB trails, or MTB trails up to level 2, 3 or 4
- My ride types: save and name your own settings, make one Go's default, and share them
- Routing: pleasure, training, Group Ride and Cargo rides steer around waits at lights more, and sharp turns count extra
- New sliders for waits and sharp turns, plus a Fewer turns switch
- Fast: as direct as possible
- Go: your route stays put until you tap Edit route; Roam is under More, and the map key is in plain words
- New map: the Rockville Pike path through NIH, and short footway links between bike paths
