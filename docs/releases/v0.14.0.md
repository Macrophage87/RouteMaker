**RouteMaker 0.14.0**
- Go is simpler: the route sheet leads with the answer, one Start ride button and a plain stress line; on a phone, Settings, Layers and the place lists open as one-screen popups
- Where to? lists the nearest places first, with their distance; Eat has a Grocery chip
- Tap the map in Go to see that spot's address and what's nearby, with a big Route here button
- Rides: clearer buttons, Go round for a closed road, Reroute and Keep going up top when off route, and Detour's places on the map
- One Accessibility mode turns on high contrast, large ride text and map tools together
- Mountain Bike shows trails at every zoom; Mountain Bike, Gravel and Mountain Goat now plan on their own trail maps
- Joining a Share live ride offers the leader's route in one popup: Download GPX, or Send to Garmin or Wahoo if connected
- Go and Ride in 19 more of DC's languages, from Amharic to Vietnamese (Plan and the route's directions stay in English): first drafts, still to be checked by native speakers
