**RouteMaker 0.14.0**
- Go is simpler: the route sheet leads with the answer, one Start ride button and a plain stress line
- Where to? lists the nearest places first, with their distance; Eat has a Grocery chip
- Tap the map in Go to see that spot's address and what's nearby, with a big Route here button
- Settings, Ride type, Layers, Save & load and the Eat, Water and Restroom lists open as one-screen popups
- Rides: clearer buttons, Go round for a closed road, Reroute and Keep going up top when off route, and Detour's places on the map
- One Accessibility mode turns on high contrast, large ride text and map tools together
- Joining a broadcast ride offers Download GPX, Send to Garmin and Send to Wahoo in one popup
- Go and Ride in 19 more of DC's languages, from Amharic to Vietnamese: first drafts, still to be checked by native speakers
