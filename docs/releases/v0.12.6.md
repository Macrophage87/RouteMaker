**New**
- Opening a broadcast on a phone goes straight into Ride mode. A Share ride or Broadcast link starts its ride, and a Roam broadcast starts Roam, with the leader's dot on the map from the start. Only the leader is asked about closures; those following a broadcast are not, on opening it or on Start ride. On a computer the link still opens so you can watch the leader, as before. The ride starts without a tap, so an iPhone may stay quiet until you first tap the screen.
- Broadcast by QR code: hold Broadcast (beside Start, on the ride screen, or in Roam), or right-click it, and the broadcast link shows as a big QR code for riders nearby to scan with their phone's camera. Held beside Start, the pop-up also has Start ride. It is the same link the share sheet sends: pressing Broadcast or holding it again during a ride gives the same link every time.
- Riders following a broadcast can pass it on: their Broadcast, or its QR code, sends the link they opened, so everyone who joins follows the same leader.
- Links open in the phone app: on Android, a tapped link to the site's map, a ride type or a shared route opens the RouteMaker app from the Play Store instead of the browser, broadcast links included. The privacy policy, the guides and Featured routes posts still open in the browser. Install the app's next store build after the site is updated, since Android checks the link when the app is installed.

**Beta**
- The site serves `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (no password) from `ANDROID_APP_ID` and `ANDROID_CERT_SHA256` in `.env`; either unset, it is a 404 and links open the browser as before. The nginx site gains that one public path, so the ship re-renders nginx. No database changes, no map rebuild needed.
