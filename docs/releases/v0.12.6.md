**New**
- Opening a broadcast on a phone goes straight into Ride mode. A Share ride or Broadcast link starts its ride, and a Roam broadcast starts Roam, with the leader's dot on the map from the start. If the route runs through a closure, the closure question comes up first and Start ride is yours to press. On a computer the link still opens so you can watch the leader, as before. The ride starts without a tap, so an iPhone may stay quiet until you first tap the screen.
