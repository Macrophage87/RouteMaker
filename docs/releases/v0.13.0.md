**New**
- Spanish: Go, Ride, spoken directions and the route's own sentences in Spanish, for phones set to Spanish or by choosing it under Settings > Display > Language. Street, trail and place names and agency notices stay as written; miles stay first.
- Arrival time before you ride: Go's route sheet and Plan's totals read like "1 h 48 min · arrive 8:27 AM", leaving now. Not shown for transit or bikeshare trips, Mass Rides, or when Plan's When is set to another time.
- Riding speed: fast riders can set their own pace, 8 to 30 mph (13 to 48 km/h), in the ride settings under Hills. Route times, the arrival time and the ride's time to go follow it; hills and junction waits still count. Mass Ride and share bikes keep their own pace. Signed in, it follows you to other devices.
- Send alert is now a large yellow button on the ride screen. The number of riders, you included, shows inside Broadcast; who is where is under More > Riders. In Roam and Plan the riders chip is a person icon and a number.
- State and county lines on the map: state and District lines are a darker dash, county lines a lighter one, all under the stress lines.
- Go's route card counts stops and via points apart, so a saved commute with three via points reads "3 via points", not "3 stops".

**Better directions**
- Turn directions are clearer: the first turn after a start or stop near a corner is said, distances are spoken in feet up to just under 1,000 ft (300 m) instead of rounded tenths of a mile, and several confusing phrasings are fixed.
- Re-routing during a ride keeps every stop and, where it can, rejoins your chosen route. Via points are guides: one less than half a mile ahead is dropped as before, and the next one further on is skipped only when reaching it would now cost more than about 5 minutes over your plan, such as when a road is closed.
- Routes come back faster: repeat requests share one answer across the server, and a plan you have already replaced is cancelled instead of finished.
- On a trail that runs beside a street, the ride names the trail (Metropolitan Branch Trail, not "Blair Road Northwest bike lane").

**Fixed**
- Riding through a dead spot no longer loses the base map after a brief signal drop; the ride waits a little longer between retries instead. The confusing note about what was saved offline now says only "Map not saved offline: it needs a signal" when nothing was kept.

**Closures**
- Bonifant St in Silver Spring (Ramsey Ave to Georgia Ave) is closed for Purple Line work through March 31, 2027, red on the map and routed around.
- With the next map update: the dirt path between Elkhart St and 3rd Ave in Silver Spring is closed and no longer drawn as a trail, and the walkways at rail platforms that allow bikes (such as the ramp to Kensington's MARC platform) are walked, not ridden as bike paths.

**Beta**
- Hand ship: adds a small memory-only cache service (memcached: 16 MB of answers, 24 MB memory limit) shared by the api's workers, and the api image gains pymemcache, so the api image is rebuilt and the beta compose file changes. No database migrations. The map items above need the next map rebuild and the overrides loaded.
