**New**
- Arrival times on the ride: under the turn, the distance and time to the next stop and to the end now end with the clock time you should get there ("8 min · 6:42 AM", in your phone's usual 12 or 24 hour clock). After about half a mile (800 m) the times follow your own pace, fully by about 3 mi (5 km); a long stop, time off the route, paused or walking doesn't count against you. Where am I? says it too: "1.2 miles to stop 2, arriving about 6:42 AM." The time still ahead counts the hills and the junction waits (signals, stop signs, yields) where they are on the route, not spread evenly over the distance. A route planned before this update keeps the even spread.
- More on the ride screen now opens a row of round buttons beside it, one press each: Less traffic, Fewer hills, Skip this stop (with the stop's number) and a Settings gear for the ride's settings. Only one side menu is open at a time.
- Broadcast works everywhere: where there is no share sheet (some computer browsers, and the Android app until its update), it copies the link, starts the ride and says "Broadcast link copied. Paste it to your riders". If the copy fails, nothing starts and it says so.
- Android app update: Broadcast, Share route and Share ride open Android's own share sheet. Android doesn't say when you back out of the sheet, so that still counts as sent.
- A shared link's preview in chat apps now draws the route on a map of the streets, parks and water around it, with the map's credits. Around your Home only the route is left out; the map stays.
- Featured routes posts can carry up to 20 photos, under About this ride or a highlight, each with alt text and an optional caption. Photos are made smaller and saved again before they go up, so no location, date or camera details go with them.
- The walks on the Washington Monument grounds and in Constitution Gardens are open to bikes. The memorials, the Monument's plaza, the Tidal Basin's shore walks and any walk that runs into a memorial stay closed, as the National Mall's rules ask you to walk your bike there.
- Closures from DDOT's big projects: routes go round the closures that DDOT posts as project traffic advisories, which rarely reach its permit or alert feeds. They show red on the map, in the closure check and in saved-route alerts. The first ones: the Connecticut Avenue NW service lanes at Dupont Circle for the deckover (the 1600 block northbound lane, Q to R St, through about Nov 3), and the Theodore Roosevelt Bridge trail, closed until about September 2027, with detours over Key Bridge and Memorial Bridge.
- In the ride type dialog, Cargo Bike's two choices (Carrying cargo, Cargo with passengers) sit side by side with a short summary each, and the next card moves up beside them. A screen reader still hears each choice's full description.

**Fixed**
- On a Featured routes post, Open in RouteMaker now opens the post's route when it was written from a GPX track, instead of an empty app.
- On a Featured routes post, Send to Garmin and Send to Wahoo now show a popup saying what happened, and a second press while one is sending does nothing, so the course is no longer sent again and again.
- The map legend no longer says trail and cobblestone changes are coming "from the next map update": they are on the map now, and Gravel and Mountain Goat also ride green (level 1) trails.

**Documentation**
- New: how a route's time is worked out, with each ride type's flat-road pace, and how routes treat sensitive areas such as memorials, bases and golf courses.
- The pages now say which map changes are live (Capitol grounds, farm tracks, golf courses, zip lines and more), and describe the arrival times, More's buttons, Broadcast's copy-link fallback and post photos.

**Beta**
- Fixes the beta's web server settings from v0.12.3, which the server refused because of the robots.txt line. Beta owner step: re-render the site file (BETA-RUNBOOK "A new release sha", step 9). Every rendered variant is now checked in CI before a release is tagged.
- The app server now reads the base map for link previews: the beta's base map folder must be in place before the app starts (it already is wherever the beta shows the map).
- Release tags whose phone app changed now build the store app and send it to Google Play's internal testing track.
