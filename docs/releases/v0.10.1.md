**Fixed**
- A sharp turn that curls round, such as a ramp up onto a bridge, is now said on the side you actually turn. Before, a hairpin right could be called a left.
- A short path across a road between two turns is now said as the crossing: "Cross Redland Road, then left onto Redland Road bike lane", on screen, by voice and to screen readers, with a straight-on arrow.
- A trail fork whose two branches both lean off (a Y) now gets its cue, such as "Bear left to stay on Rock Creek Trail". So does a trail that turns at a T.
- The Ride ended summary fits on the screen: each figure is one row, the elevation chart is sized to the screen, and Done and Undo are always in view. On a short or sideways screen only the figures scroll.
- A route to a Metro or MARC station with your own bike now ends at the elevator nearest where you're coming from, and the app checks which of the station's elevators is quickest to ride to, so it no longer sends you round the far side of the tracks. If it moves your point, it says so, and Undo puts it back. An elevator you tap on the map stays where you put it.
