**New**
- Broadcast: a small button beside Start, in Plan and Go. It opens your phone's share sheet with a link to the route; once you send it, your ride starts and you are its leader. You are shown on the map from then on, even if you leave the route, while the app is open. Turn it off any time with Show me as a dot, under the riders count.
- Share ride now makes you the ride's leader too.
- Opening a Share ride or Broadcast link follows the leader: the map moves with the leader's dot, which has a wide ring round it, and the path the leader has taken since you opened the link is drawn as a dashed line. You see the leader at once, before answering whether to show yourself. Moving the map stops following; Follow the leader, under the riders count, turns it back on. While you ride yourself, the map follows you instead. A link opened in Plan follows the leader too, so someone at home can watch the ride.
