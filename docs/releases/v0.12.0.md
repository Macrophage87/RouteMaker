**New**
- Broadcast: a small button beside Start, in Plan and Go. It opens your phone's share sheet with a link to the route; once you send it, your ride starts and you are its leader. If something goes wrong (the route isn't ready, the share failed), it says so beside the button and nothing starts. Turn your dot off any time with Show me as a dot, under the riders count.
- Share ride now makes you the ride's leader too. Sharing the same route again, to another chat, keeps you the leader for both.
- Opening a Share ride or Broadcast link follows the leader: the map moves with the leader's dot, which has a wide ring round it. The leader's phone sends the route it is riding (dashed, so a re-route shows) and the path it has taken (solid) about every 2 minutes and whenever the route changes, so someone who opens the link late sees the whole path (except the part within 650 ft (200 m) in a straight line of where it started, as rides often start at home). The server keeps these in memory for 2 minutes only. You see the leader at once, before answering whether to show yourself. Moving or zooming the map stops following; Follow the leader, under the riders count, turns it back on. While you ride yourself, the map follows you instead. A link opened in Plan follows the leader too, so someone at home can watch the ride.
- On a Share ride or Broadcast ride, everyone who chooses to show their dot is shown on or off the route while they ride, not only within a quarter mile of it, so the leader and riders who get separated can find each other. Once your ride ends, the quarter-mile rule is back.
- Roam: in Go with nothing planned, the big button is Roam, a ride with no destination. It shows the map, the road you are on and up to 3 roads ahead with their distance and stress level, read from the map already on your phone. Detour finds the nearest water, restroom or food by bike; Ride here rides there and comes back to Roam when that ride ends. Broadcast, a round button on the Roam screen, shares a link so others can follow your dot (distances are in a straight line, as there is no route). The next sunrise and sunset show in the corner. Slide End roam to stop. Your position stays on your phone, except for a Detour search and the ride it starts, and, while you broadcast, your dot and the way you've ridden, which the server keeps for 2 minutes.
- Ride alerts have two more choices on a group ride, "Lost, separated from the group" and "Wait up, regroup", and a Mass Ride has "Lost, separated from the ride".
- Alert, Other has a Speak button where your browser supports speech to text: say the issue and it fills the box; check it and tap Send. Your browser's maker (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari) turns the recording into text.
- Privacy: ride data is kept in memory only, and for 2 minutes at most once it stops being sent. Ride alerts now last 2 minutes (they were 10), and a phone's random ride code and its alert counts are forgotten 2 minutes after it last checked in.
