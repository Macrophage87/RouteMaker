RouteMaker 0.25.0
- Save a route as a Fixed route in Plan to keep its exact line; Ride never re-routes it
- Add your own notes to a route (caution or point of interest, with an icon); Ride says them as you reach them
- Give stops an icon, shown as the app's pictures or your phone's own emoji
- Plan's lock-screen cue images now have turn arrows, a new part at each stop, and a dark version for night rides
- In AI assistants that can show it, the RouteMaker connector draws the route map with stress and elevation graphs in the chat
- Ride now calls the Bethesda Trolley Trail turn at Charles Street
