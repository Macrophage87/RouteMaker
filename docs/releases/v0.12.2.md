**New**
- Work zones follow the road: a Maryland closure given only by its two ends is drawn along the street between them, not as a straight line.
- Closures on roads you can't ride a bike on, such as Interstates, are no longer listed or warned about.
- If something goes wrong, the app now shows "Something went wrong" with Reload (your plan is kept in the link) and Copy error report, instead of a black page. The report leaves out where you ride and stays on your device unless you paste it somewhere.

**AI assistants**
- Assistants connect through RouteMaker's own connector: in Claude, add a custom connector at RouteMaker's address followed by /mcp and sign in. Settings, Account, AI assistants lists your connected apps with Disconnect.
- The AI assistant key is retired. The server you run on your own computer still plans routes and writes GPX files; to keep a route, open its link and press Save. If you used a key, update your copy of the code.
- Instance admins can have their assistant write Featured routes posts as drafts, to publish in the admin.

**Fixed**
- Closing a closure card no longer blanks the page.
- A click that closes a map card no longer also picks another route or adds a point.
- Map cards now show above every marker.
- On a phone with large text, the Plan header keeps the RouteMaker name whole.
