**New**
- Go's More direct and Calmer options now show up and give a clearly different route.
- Plan has a manual mode: press the straight-line button on the map, and each point you tap is joined to the last by a straight line, for a path the planner won't take.
- You can delete your account: Settings, Account, Delete account. It says what is deleted and what is kept, and asks once.
- The ride screen's top card is smaller: the next turn in large print with the details under it, and sunrise, sunset and civil twilight in the corner when one is within the hour.
- End ride is now a slide to the right, so a bump doesn't end your ride. Once you've arrived it's a plain button again. It comes first in the row; Road closed shows as its icon where the row is short of room.

**Fixed**
- Planning on Android no longer gets stuck at keyboard height after the keyboard closes.
- Go and a ride now route around closures known to be in effect only. DDOT work permits (a road *may* be closed) stay in Plan, for planning a later ride.

**Coming with the next map update**
- Roads Maryland's highway agency (MDOT SHA) closes to bikes are left off the map and out of routes: about 100 mi (160 km) that were only marked Avoid.
- Roads Montgomery County rates its highest stress level (LTS 5) now show as LTS 4.5, a very high-stress road, instead of Avoid: not ruled out where there is no real alternative, such as Darnestown Road at Dawsonville.
