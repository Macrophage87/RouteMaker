**RouteMaker 0.18.0**
- Road Bike ride type replaces the Avoid gravel box: keeps to pavement where it can, fewer waits at lights, no winding trails
- Walk and Walk + transit ride types, with turn-by-turn on foot and transit stop by stop
- Run ride type: trails, paths and quiet streets in long stretches, fewer lights, lit ways after dark, loops sized to a target distance
- Walks avoid crossing busy roads away from signals, and slip lanes; an Avoid stairs switch in Settings
- Transit rides are rechecked with live times until you board; a quicker trip takes over, delays are said
- The AI assistant connector can tune long rides and commutes, and lists closures along a route
- Long routes plan a little faster
