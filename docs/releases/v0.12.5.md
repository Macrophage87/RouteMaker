**New**
- Favorites: signed in, Go's Home button becomes Favorites. It opens Home, then Work once you set it, then your other places (up to 20 besides Home); one tap rides there. Set Work and Add a place search right in the list, and Edit changes Home or removes a place. Holding a spot on the map and the end-of-ride summary both offer Save to Favorites. Favorites are kept with your account only, never on the device or in a log, and are deleted with it. Signed out, nothing changes.
- Saved routes in Go: More, then Saved routes, opens the same list as Plan's Load route. One tap on a route opens it in Go, ready to Start ride.
- Topo lines on the ride: More on the ride screen has a Topo lines button that turns contour lines on and off mid-ride. It is the same switch as Map layers' Topo lines.
- Steep grades on Mass Ride and Novice and Nervous: stretches steeper than 5% either way, at least 100 m (about 330 ft) long, get an orange marker, and steeper than 8% a red one. The marker's ramp rises for uphill and falls for downhill, with the grade beside it ("+9%", "-6%") when you zoom in. Routes on those two ride types now steer away from them, red ones more than orange.
- Turns in quick succession: when up to three turns come within 100 m (about 330 ft) of each other, the ride card draws them as one arrow. The early warning says all of them; at the junction each turn gets its own "now", the next one once you're past the one before.
- The ride map turns to the way your phone faces from the start: before GPS knows your direction (it only does once you are moving), track-up uses the phone's compass. On an iPhone, the first Start ride or Roam asks to use motion and orientation; say Allow for this, or the map stays north-up until you start moving, as before.
- Crossings of busy multi-lane roads: where you stop or give way to a road with two or more lanes each way (three or more on a one-way), routes now count its traffic, not just its bike lane. Sheridan St NW across Piney Branch Rd NW is one: a four-lane, 30 mph road that counted as calm because of its painted lanes.
