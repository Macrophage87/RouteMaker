RouteMaker 0.26.0
- Ride warns you when police activity or a crash in DC, or a Montgomery County road closure, blocks your route, and offers a new route
- With no signal, Ride shows how far away your planned route is and draws a line back to it
- Plan a loop from just a start and a distance: pick one of up to three shapes
- Routes take fewer needless turns, and each one shows its turns, waits at lights and stops, and unpaved miles, with Fewer turns and All paved options
- Cargo and Tandem routes go round stiles, steps and narrow gates, and mark the ones left on the map
- Closed sites such as NASA Goddard are shaded on the map, and a route to one ends at its gate; big parks such as Rock Creek Park now have their names on the map
- Go's first screen puts warnings near the top, and Where to? lists Home, Work and your favorites
- After a ride, you can tell us which streets felt different from their rating
