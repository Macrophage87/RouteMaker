**RouteMaker 0.22.0**
- Saved routes take tags, and an Archive keeps old ones out of the way (Load route, in Plan and Go)
- Rebalance Ride needs no destination: it finds the nearest full dock, then shows where to drop off when you get there, and totals your day
- Bikeshare and Rebalance Ride take up to 3 stops, each a new trip
- Mountain-bike trails show in their trail colours on the ride ribbon and the route line
- Stop for lunch: within 2 hours, Resume carries on the same ride quietly
- A quieter map when zoomed out, and Fairfax park trail closures on the map
- Clearer turn arrows where you cross and then turn, and one left through a junction's crosswalk
- Featured routes keep where each photo was taken
