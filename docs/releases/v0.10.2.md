**Fixed**
- Own bike + transit: the trips are now ranked by arrival time plus a penalty for time on busy roads (LTS 3 and up) and junction waits, and re-ranked once the chosen trip's rides are routed, so a trip listed first is never one that is both slower and busier than one below it. Each trip shows its busy-road distance ("0.4 mi (0.6 km) busy" or "no busy roads"), which explains when they are not in pure time order. The times shown stay real times.
- Own bike + transit: every Metro station takes a bike. NoMa and 26 other stations were treated as closed to bikes because no elevator was on file; they have walk-in entrances. Steps say "by the elevator" only where one is known.
- Own bike + transit: a street elevator out at a station with no spare (or a platform elevator out) adds 15 min to that trip's ranking score only, not its time, and the trip shows an elevator-out icon. A station with a working spare elevator is not penalised.
- Plan: tapping another trip (B, C, D) now plans it at once, and the pick survives a fresh plan.
- Own bike + transit: the header distance, climb, descent and elevation chart now cover every ride of the trip, not only the first.

Everything in v0.10.1 is in this release too: see [v0.10.1](v0.10.1.md).
