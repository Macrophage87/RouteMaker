**Fixed**
- 311 while riding: the 311 menu shows all six problems at once, and one tap logs it, with no note to type. Suggest is now called Reports (in Plan's bottom bar and Go's Menu); your 311 checklist there shows when each problem was logged and when it was reported, and one left unreported for 30 days moves to Archived by itself. After End ride, the summary says how many problems are still to report to 311 and has Open Reports.
- Own bike + transit: trips are now ranked by when you get there, with time on busy roads and waiting at junctions counting against a trip, and ranked again once the chosen trip is routed. Each trip says how far you would ride on busy roads ("0.4 mi (0.6 km) busy" or "no busy roads"), which explains when the list is not in strict time order. The times shown stay real times.
- Own bike + transit: every Metro station takes a bike. NoMa and 26 other stations were treated as closed to bikes because no elevator was on file; they have walk-in entrances. Steps say "by the elevator" only where one is known.
- Own bike + transit: a street elevator out at a station with no spare (or a platform elevator out) adds 15 min to that trip's ranking score only, not its time, and the trip shows an elevator-out icon. A station with a working spare elevator is not penalized, and elevators in a station's parking garage or lot (such as Glenmont's) are ignored: they are not on the way to the platform.
- Plan: tapping another trip (B, C, D) now plans it at once, and the pick survives a fresh plan.
- Own bike + transit: the header distance, climb, descent and elevation chart now cover every ride of the trip, not only the first.
- Map: neighborhood and town names are now in an extra-bold Atkinson Hyperlegible (the street names' font family), dark on a white outline, instead of thin, widely spaced grey capitals. Towns are in normal case and a little larger; neighborhoods are in small capitals, so they read apart from street names.
- Map: Metro and MARC station names are larger than street names (18 px, 24 px while riding) and drawn over pins and the route, so they stay the most prominent names on the map (brought forward from v0.11.0).
- Capitol grounds: the walks on the US Capitol grounds, in the Senate Parks and round Union Square are open to bikes, as the Capitol Police Board's rules allow, so routes can cross the grounds and end on them. Needs the next map build.
- Map: park, water, island and house-number names, route numbers, and the app's own names (mountain-bike trails, scenic spots, waypoints, route choices, mile markers, contour heights) are now in the street names' bold Atkinson Hyperlegible on a white outline. Numbers inside map icons (climbs, bike docks) keep their size so they still fit. Parks stay green and water blue, in darker shades.
- Map: at the two zooms where every street first appears, plain streets in a dense city grid are drawn thinner, so downtown reads as a mesh instead of a solid block of colour. Rural and suburban roads, bike lanes, paths, trails and unpaved roads keep their full width. No map rebuild needed; road tiles redraw once after the update.

Everything in v0.10.1 is in this release too: see [v0.10.1](v0.10.1.md).
