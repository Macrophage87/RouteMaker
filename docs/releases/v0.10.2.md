**New**
- In Plan, once your route is up to date and you've picked it, the big button at the top of the map becomes Save route, with Start ride beside it. On a phone they read Save and Start. Change anything and it's Update route again.
- Save route's box also has Download GPX. Not signed in, it offers Sign in with Discord (your plan is kept) or Download GPX.
- A route someone sent you by link can be saved to My routes. Not signed in, Save offers Sign in with Discord or Download GPX.
- With nothing planned yet, that button is Load route, which opens Save & load.
- Plan's Food, Water and Places to see buttons on the map are now behind one Find button, leaving more of the map clear.
- On a computer, Start ride first checks that you mean it, since it's made for a phone on your bike. Go back, or Start anyway (not asked again in that tab).
