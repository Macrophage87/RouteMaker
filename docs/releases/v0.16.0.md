**RouteMaker 0.16.0**
- Road ratings recalibrated: Maryland state roads, West Virginia county roads and Q, R and 1st Streets NW rechecked, with half steps (3.5, 4.5) where a road sits between levels
- Half steps get their own colours on routes, the map and the legend: orange for 3.5, red for 4, dark red for 4.5, and Avoid in magenta
- Gravel roads with tight bends or one lane are read at 25 mph, so they rate calmer than straight gravel
- Go: tap the microphone in Where to? and say a place, then say a letter (B or Bravo) to pick one of the places found
- Go: when your phone's first location is rough (miles out), the dot shows its circle and corrects itself once the phone knows better
