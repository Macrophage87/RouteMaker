**RouteMaker 0.16.0**
- Road ratings recalibrated: Maryland state roads, West Virginia roads, Northern Virginia streets and DC streets rechecked (busy DC roads no longer rated as calm as the path beside them), with half steps (such as 2.5 and 3.5) where a road sits between levels
- Busy city streets rate from their traffic: half a level higher from 12,000 cars a day in town and a full level from 20,000, and busy two-lane country highways 4.5; Virginia streets that bend now get their traffic counts
- New colours: LTS 4 is now red and Avoid magenta; half steps get their own (3.5 orange, 4.5 dark red, new greens for 1.5 and 2.5)
- Rural gravel roads with tight bends or one lane are read at 25 mph, so they can rate calmer than straight gravel
- Go: tap the microphone in Where to? and say a place, then say a letter (B or Bravo) to pick one of the places found
- Go: when your phone's first location is rough (miles out), the dot shows its circle and corrects itself once the phone knows better
