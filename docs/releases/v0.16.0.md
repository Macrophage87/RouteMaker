**RouteMaker 0.16.0**
- Road ratings recalibrated in DC, Maryland, Northern Virginia and West Virginia, with half steps (such as 2.5 and 3.5) where a road sits between levels
- Busy streets rate higher from their traffic counts, and busy two-lane country highways rate 4.5
- Junctions are priced by the traffic you cross (speed, lanes and count), so a bike lane on a busy road no longer makes it look easy to cross; half-step roads cost halfway between their levels
- New colours: LTS 4 is now red and Avoid magenta; half steps get their own (3.5 orange, 4.5 dark red, new greens for 1.5 and 2.5)
- Rural gravel roads with tight bends or one lane are read at 25 mph, so they can rate calmer than straight gravel
- Go: tap the microphone in Where to? and say a place, then, in English or Spanish, say a letter (B or Bravo) to pick one of the places found
- Go: when your phone's first location is rough (more than about 0.6 mile [1 km] out), the dot shows its circle and corrects itself once the phone knows better
- Light times now say Dawn and Dusk instead of "civil twilight begins/ends", and on the ride screen End ride and Go round share one row, their words smaller where a long word would not fit
