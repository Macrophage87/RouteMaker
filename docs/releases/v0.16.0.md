**RouteMaker 0.16.0**
- Road ratings recalibrated: Maryland state roads, West Virginia roads and Q, R and 1st Streets NW rechecked, with half steps (such as 2.5 and 3.5) where a road sits between levels
- New colours: LTS 4 is now red and Avoid magenta; half steps get their own (3.5 orange, 4.5 dark red, new greens for 1.5 and 2.5)
- Rural gravel roads with tight bends or one lane are read at 25 mph, so they can rate calmer than straight gravel
- Go: tap the microphone in Where to? and say a place, then say a letter (B or Bravo) to pick one of the places found
- Go: when your phone's first location is rough (miles out), the dot shows its circle and corrects itself once the phone knows better
