**RouteMaker 0.16.1**
- Share live: your dot now goes out from the ride's own GPS, so riders on the Android app and in a browser see each other again
- Share live and Group/Mass Ride dots update every 5 seconds instead of 15, and every 2 seconds while 20 or fewer share (such as a big ride's marshals), so slowdowns show sooner
- Send alert: when an alert does not go, the reason now shows in the alert panel, not only to screen readers
