TRAFFIC ADVISORY: Street Closures for the Labor Day Concert on the National Mall

Friday, September 4, 2026

(Washington, DC) – The Metropolitan Police Department wishes to notify motorists of the following parking restrictions and street closures for the Labor Day concert on the National Mall on Monday, September 7, 2026.

The following streets will be closed from 4:30 am to 1:30 pm:

  • Constitution Avenue from 14th Street to 18th Street, NW
  • 15th Street, NW from Constitution Avenue to Independence Avenue, SW
  • 17th Street, NW between Constitution Avenue and E Street, NW

The following streets will be closed from 2:00 pm on Monday, September 7, 2026 to 2:00 am on Tuesday, September 8, 2026:

  • Madison Drive, NW from 3rd Street to 14th Street, NW
  • Jefferson Drive, SW from 3rd Street to 14th Street, SW

Emergency No Parking will be in effect from 12:01 am to 3:00 pm:

  • Constitution Avenue, NW from 7th Street to 15th Street, NW
  • Both sides of Virginia Avenue, NW from 18th Street to 23rd Street, NW

On Saturday, September 5, 2026, the following street may be closed intermittently:

  • Independence Avenue, SW from 3rd Street to 7th Street, SW

Motorists are reminded that parking restrictions will be strictly enforced. Additional closures may occur at the discretion of the Metropolitan Police Department. For live updates, follow @DCPoliceTraffic on X.
